# Wallpaper

`wallpaper.jpg`, `wallpaper-1600.jpg` and the desktop behind the windows in `v2/today-*.png` are KDE Plasma's
"Waterfall" wallpaper by Krystian Zajdel (Plasma's `wallpapers/Next`), licensed CC-BY-SA-4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). They are cropped and resized; the `today` pictures are
off-screen captures with the desktop behind their windows replaced by it. These files are shared under the
same license.
